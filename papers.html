Key papers

The studies that define what we know

Grouped by topic, from foundational genetics to treatment. Within each topic, papers are listed oldest first. Click a citation to open the paper.

Genetics and risk

  • Corder EH, Saunders AM, Strittmatter WJ, et al. Gene dose of apolipoprotein E type 4 allele and the risk of Alzheimer’s disease in late onset families. Science 1993.

    First showed that risk rises and age at onset falls with each ε4 copy.

  • Strittmatter WJ, Saunders AM, Schmechel D, et al. Apolipoprotein E: high-avidity binding to β-amyloid and increased frequency of type 4 allele in late-onset familial Alzheimer disease. PNAS 1993.

    Linked APOE to Alzheimer’s and to amyloid binding.

  • Farrer LA, Cupples LA, Haines JL, et al. Effects of age, sex, and ethnicity on the association between apolipoprotein E genotype and Alzheimer disease: a meta-analysis. JAMA 1997.

    Classic estimates of risk by genotype, age, sex and ancestry.

  • Genin E, Hannequin D, Wallon D, et al. APOE and Alzheimer disease: a major gene with semi-dominant inheritance. Molecular Psychiatry 2011.

    Lifetime risk estimates for ε4/ε4.

  • Belloy ME, Andrews SJ, Le Guen Y, et al. APOE genotype and Alzheimer disease risk across age, sex, and population ancestry. JAMA Neurology 2023.

    Large modern estimates across ancestries.

  • Fortea J, Pegueroles J, Alcolea D, et al. APOE4 homozygosity represents a distinct genetic form of Alzheimer’s disease. Nature Medicine 2024.

    Near-full penetrance of pathology and predictable onset in ε4/ε4.

Amyloid, blood vessels and bleeding

  • Greenberg SM, Rebeck GW, Vonsattel JP, Gomez-Isla T, Hyman BT. Apolipoprotein E ε4 and cerebral hemorrhage associated with amyloid angiopathy. Annals of Neurology 1995.

    About 13-fold higher odds of moderate to severe amyloid angiopathy with two ε4 copies.

  • Jansen WJ, Ossenkoppele R, Knol DL, et al. Prevalence of cerebral amyloid pathology in persons without dementia: a meta-analysis. JAMA 2015.

    Amyloid positivity by age and APOE genotype in people without dementia.

  • Montagne A, Nation DA, Sagare AP, et al. APOE4 leads to blood–brain barrier dysfunction predicting cognitive decline. Nature 2020.

    A vascular route for APOE4 effects, independent of amyloid and tau.

  • Independent effects of white matter lesion volume and APOE ε4 on ARIA-H in the A4 Study. Alzheimer’s & Dementia 2025.

    ε4/ε4 predicts accumulating small bleeds in people without symptoms.

    See trial: A4 StudySee resource: A4 data

Blood biomarkers

  • APOE ε4 carriership determines a faster plasma p-tau217 progression in Aβ-positive individuals. Alzheimer’s & Dementia 2025.

    In amyloid-positive people, p-tau217 rises faster in ε4 carriers (TRIAD and ADNI).

    See resource: ADNI
  • APOE4 impact on soluble and insoluble tau pathology is mostly influenced by amyloid-β. Brain 2025.

    A contrasting view: in BioFINDER and WRAP, APOE4 effects on p-tau217 largely run through amyloid.

Mechanisms

  • Shi Y, Yamada K, Liddelow SA, et al. ApoE4 markedly exacerbates tau-mediated neurodegeneration in a mouse model of tauopathy. Nature 2017.

    APOE4 worsens tau damage, partly through microglia.

  • Lin YT, Seo J, Gao F, et al. APOE4 causes widespread molecular and cellular alterations associated with Alzheimer’s disease phenotypes in human iPSC-derived brain cell types. Neuron 2018.

    Matched human cell models of APOE3 versus APOE4.

  • Blanchard JW, Akay LA, Davila-Velderrain J, et al. APOE4 impairs myelination via cholesterol dysregulation in oligodendrocytes. Nature 2022.

    Cholesterol handling and myelin loss in human ε4 brains and cells.

  • Haney MS, Pálovics R, Munson CN, et al. APOE4/4 is linked to damaging lipid droplets in Alzheimer’s disease microglia. Nature 2024.

    Lipid-laden microglia in ε4/ε4 brains.

  • Akay L, …, Tsai LH. Genome-wide CRISPR screen reveals Wnt signaling as a potential therapeutic target for lipid accumulation and impaired myelination in APOE4 oligodendrocytes. Cell Stem Cell 2026.

    APOE4 suppresses Wnt signaling in myelin-making cells; blocking GSK3β cleared lipid droplets and restored myelination in human brain organoids and mice.

Protection and resilience

  • Arboleda-Velasquez JF, Lopera F, O’Hare M, et al. Resistance to autosomal dominant Alzheimer’s disease in an APOE3 Christchurch homozygote: a case report. Nature Medicine 2019.

    A rare APOE variant that appears to protect against early-onset disease, pointing to APOE as a drug target.

  • Le Guen Y, et al. Association of rare APOE missense variants V236E and R251G with risk of Alzheimer disease. JAMA Neurology 2022.

    R251G, inherited together with ε4, roughly halves its risk effect, showing that ε4 risk can be offset.

  • Nelson MR, et al. The APOE-R136S mutation protects against APOE4-driven Tau pathology, neurodegeneration and neuroinflammation. Nature Neuroscience 2023.

    The Christchurch change blocks APOE4-driven tau damage in mice, supporting APOE-directed therapies.

Targeting APOE

  • Huynh TV, et al. Age-dependent effects of apoE reduction using antisense oligonucleotides in a model of β-amyloidosis. Neuron 2017.

    Lowering APOE before plaques form strongly reduces amyloid pathology in mice; later lowering changes plaque size and toxicity.

  • Liao F, et al. Targeting of nonlipidated, aggregated apoE with antibodies inhibits amyloid accumulation. Journal of Clinical Investigation 2018.

    The HAE-4 antibody, which binds aggregated apoE, lowers plaque burden in APOE4 mice.

Trial results by genotype

  • van Dyck CH, Swanson CJ, Aisen P, et al. Lecanemab in early Alzheimer’s disease. New England Journal of Medicine 2023.

    Clarity AD; ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

    See trial: Clarity ADSee drug: lecanemab
  • Sims JR, Zimmer JA, Evans CD, et al. Donanemab in early symptomatic Alzheimer disease: the TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 randomized clinical trial. JAMA 2023.

    ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

    See trial: TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2See drug: donanemab
  • Efficacy and safety of oral valiltramiprosate in APOE4/4 homozygotes with early AD: topline results from the APOLLOE4 Phase 3 trial. 2025.

    The first completed Phase 3 trial run only in ε4/ε4.

    See trial: APOLLOE4See drug: valiltramiprosate
  • Safety and ARIA results of the oral anti-amyloid agent valiltramiprosate from the Phase 3 APOLLOE4 trial in APOE4/4 homozygotes with early AD. 2025.

    ARIA-E about 3% on drug and placebo, with no symptomatic ARIA over 78 weeks, in a group with high amyloid-angiopathy burden.

    See trial: APOLLOE4See drug: valiltramiprosate
  • Real-world use of lecanemab in APOE ε4 homozygotes and in patients on antithrombotic therapy. 2025.

    Interim clinic data: ARIA-E in 17% of ε4/ε4 patients treated outside trials.

    See drug: lecanemab
  • Abushakra S, …, Tolar M. Full peer-reviewed results of the APOLLOE4 Phase 3 trial of oral valiltramiprosate in APOE4/4 homozygotes with early Alzheimer’s disease. Drugs 2025.

    No significant clinical benefit at 78 weeks overall, but slower brain atrophy; MCI-subgroup benefits were not the primary analysis.

    See trial: APOLLOE4See drug: valiltramiprosate
