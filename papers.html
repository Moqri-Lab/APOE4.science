Key papers

The studies that define what we know

Grouped by topic. “Find” opens the paper or a PubMed search for it.

Genetics and risk

  • Corder EH, Saunders AM, Strittmatter WJ, et al. Gene dose of apolipoprotein E type 4 allele and the risk of Alzheimer’s disease in late onset families. Science 1993. Find

    First showed that risk rises and age at onset falls with each ε4 copy.

  • Strittmatter WJ, Saunders AM, Schmechel D, et al. Apolipoprotein E: high-avidity binding to β-amyloid and increased frequency of type 4 allele in late-onset familial Alzheimer disease. PNAS 1993. Find

    Linked APOE to Alzheimer’s and to amyloid binding.

  • Farrer LA, Cupples LA, Haines JL, et al. Effects of age, sex, and ethnicity on the association between apolipoprotein E genotype and Alzheimer disease: a meta-analysis. JAMA 1997. Find

    Classic estimates of risk by genotype, age, sex and ancestry.

  • Genin E, Hannequin D, Wallon D, et al. APOE and Alzheimer disease: a major gene with semi-dominant inheritance. Molecular Psychiatry 2011. Find

    Lifetime risk estimates for ε4/ε4.

  • Belloy ME, Andrews SJ, Le Guen Y, et al. APOE genotype and Alzheimer disease risk across age, sex, and population ancestry. JAMA Neurology 2023. Find

    Large modern estimates across ancestries.

  • Fortea J, Pegueroles J, Alcolea D, et al. APOE4 homozygosity represents a distinct genetic form of Alzheimer’s disease. Nature Medicine 2024. Find

    Near-full penetrance of pathology and predictable onset in ε4/ε4.

Amyloid, blood vessels and bleeding

  • Greenberg SM, Rebeck GW, Vonsattel JP, Gomez-Isla T, Hyman BT. Apolipoprotein E ε4 and cerebral hemorrhage associated with amyloid angiopathy. Annals of Neurology 1995. Find

    About 13-fold higher odds of moderate to severe amyloid angiopathy with two ε4 copies.

  • Jansen WJ, Ossenkoppele R, Knol DL, et al. Prevalence of cerebral amyloid pathology in persons without dementia: a meta-analysis. JAMA 2015. Find

    Amyloid positivity by age and APOE genotype in people without dementia.

  • Montagne A, Nation DA, Sagare AP, et al. APOE4 leads to blood–brain barrier dysfunction predicting cognitive decline. Nature 2020. Find

    A vascular route for APOE4 effects, independent of amyloid and tau.

  • Independent effects of white matter lesion volume and APOE ε4 on ARIA-H in the A4 Study. Alzheimer’s & Dementia 2025. Find

    ε4/ε4 predicts accumulating small bleeds in people without symptoms.

Mechanisms

  • Shi Y, Yamada K, Liddelow SA, et al. ApoE4 markedly exacerbates tau-mediated neurodegeneration in a mouse model of tauopathy. Nature 2017. Find

    APOE4 worsens tau damage, partly through microglia.

  • Lin YT, Seo J, Gao F, et al. APOE4 causes widespread molecular and cellular alterations associated with Alzheimer’s disease phenotypes in human iPSC-derived brain cell types. Neuron 2018. Find

    Matched human cell models of APOE3 versus APOE4.

  • Blanchard JW, Akay LA, Davila-Velderrain J, et al. APOE4 impairs myelination via cholesterol dysregulation in oligodendrocytes. Nature 2022. Find

    Cholesterol handling and myelin loss in human ε4 brains and cells.

  • Haney MS, Pálovics R, Munson CN, et al. APOE4/4 is linked to damaging lipid droplets in Alzheimer’s disease microglia. Nature 2024. Find

    Lipid-laden microglia in ε4/ε4 brains.

Protection and resilience

  • Arboleda-Velasquez JF, Lopera F, O’Hare M, et al. Resistance to autosomal dominant Alzheimer’s disease in an APOE3 Christchurch homozygote: a case report. Nature Medicine 2019. Find

    A rare APOE variant that appears to protect against early-onset disease, pointing to APOE as a drug target.

Reviews

  • Belloy ME, Napolioni V, Greicius MD. A quarter century of APOE and Alzheimer’s disease: progress to date and the path forward. Neuron 2019. Find

    Broad review of APOE genetics and biology.

  • Yamazaki Y, Zhao N, Caulfield TR, Liu CC, Bu G. Apolipoprotein E and Alzheimer disease: pathobiology and targeting strategies. Nature Reviews Neurology 2019. Find

    Mechanisms and therapeutic approaches.

Trial results by genotype

  • van Dyck CH, Swanson CJ, Aisen P, et al. Lecanemab in early Alzheimer’s disease. New England Journal of Medicine 2023. Find

    Clarity AD; ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

  • Sims JR, Zimmer JA, Evans CD, et al. Donanemab in early symptomatic Alzheimer disease: the TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 randomized clinical trial. JAMA 2023. Find

    ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

  • Efficacy and safety of oral valiltramiprosate in APOE4/4 homozygotes with early AD: topline results from the APOLLOE4 Phase 3 trial. 2025. Find

    The first completed Phase 3 trial run only in ε4/ε4.
