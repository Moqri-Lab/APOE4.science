Key papers

The studies that define what we know

Grouped by topic. Each link opens the paper.

Genetics and risk

  • Corder EH, Saunders AM, Strittmatter WJ, et al. Gene dose of apolipoprotein E type 4 allele and the risk of Alzheimer’s disease in late onset families. Science 1993. Read

    First showed that risk rises and age at onset falls with each ε4 copy.

  • Strittmatter WJ, Saunders AM, Schmechel D, et al. Apolipoprotein E: high-avidity binding to β-amyloid and increased frequency of type 4 allele in late-onset familial Alzheimer disease. PNAS 1993. Read

    Linked APOE to Alzheimer’s and to amyloid binding.

  • Farrer LA, Cupples LA, Haines JL, et al. Effects of age, sex, and ethnicity on the association between apolipoprotein E genotype and Alzheimer disease: a meta-analysis. JAMA 1997. Read

    Classic estimates of risk by genotype, age, sex and ancestry.

  • Genin E, Hannequin D, Wallon D, et al. APOE and Alzheimer disease: a major gene with semi-dominant inheritance. Molecular Psychiatry 2011. Read

    Lifetime risk estimates for ε4/ε4.

  • Belloy ME, Andrews SJ, Le Guen Y, et al. APOE genotype and Alzheimer disease risk across age, sex, and population ancestry. JAMA Neurology 2023. Read

    Large modern estimates across ancestries.

  • Fortea J, Pegueroles J, Alcolea D, et al. APOE4 homozygosity represents a distinct genetic form of Alzheimer’s disease. Nature Medicine 2024. Read

    Near-full penetrance of pathology and predictable onset in ε4/ε4.

Amyloid, blood vessels and bleeding

  • Greenberg SM, Rebeck GW, Vonsattel JP, Gomez-Isla T, Hyman BT. Apolipoprotein E ε4 and cerebral hemorrhage associated with amyloid angiopathy. Annals of Neurology 1995. Read

    About 13-fold higher odds of moderate to severe amyloid angiopathy with two ε4 copies.

  • Jansen WJ, Ossenkoppele R, Knol DL, et al. Prevalence of cerebral amyloid pathology in persons without dementia: a meta-analysis. JAMA 2015. Read

    Amyloid positivity by age and APOE genotype in people without dementia.

  • Montagne A, Nation DA, Sagare AP, et al. APOE4 leads to blood–brain barrier dysfunction predicting cognitive decline. Nature 2020. Read

    A vascular route for APOE4 effects, independent of amyloid and tau.

  • Independent effects of white matter lesion volume and APOE ε4 on ARIA-H in the A4 Study. Alzheimer’s & Dementia 2025. Read

    ε4/ε4 predicts accumulating small bleeds in people without symptoms.

Blood biomarkers

  • APOE ε4 carriership determines a faster plasma p-tau217 progression in Aβ-positive individuals. Alzheimer’s & Dementia 2025. Read

    In amyloid-positive people, p-tau217 rises faster in ε4 carriers (TRIAD and ADNI).

  • APOE4 impact on soluble and insoluble tau pathology is mostly influenced by amyloid-β. Brain 2025. Read

    A contrasting view: in BioFINDER and WRAP, APOE4 effects on p-tau217 largely run through amyloid.

Mechanisms

  • Shi Y, Yamada K, Liddelow SA, et al. ApoE4 markedly exacerbates tau-mediated neurodegeneration in a mouse model of tauopathy. Nature 2017. Read

    APOE4 worsens tau damage, partly through microglia.

  • Lin YT, Seo J, Gao F, et al. APOE4 causes widespread molecular and cellular alterations associated with Alzheimer’s disease phenotypes in human iPSC-derived brain cell types. Neuron 2018. Read

    Matched human cell models of APOE3 versus APOE4.

  • Blanchard JW, Akay LA, Davila-Velderrain J, et al. APOE4 impairs myelination via cholesterol dysregulation in oligodendrocytes. Nature 2022. Read

    Cholesterol handling and myelin loss in human ε4 brains and cells.

  • Haney MS, Pálovics R, Munson CN, et al. APOE4/4 is linked to damaging lipid droplets in Alzheimer’s disease microglia. Nature 2024. Read

    Lipid-laden microglia in ε4/ε4 brains.

Protection and resilience

  • Arboleda-Velasquez JF, Lopera F, O’Hare M, et al. Resistance to autosomal dominant Alzheimer’s disease in an APOE3 Christchurch homozygote: a case report. Nature Medicine 2019. Read

    A rare APOE variant that appears to protect against early-onset disease, pointing to APOE as a drug target.

  • Le Guen Y, et al. Association of rare APOE missense variants V236E and R251G with risk of Alzheimer disease. JAMA Neurology 2022. Read

    R251G, inherited together with ε4, roughly halves its risk effect, showing that ε4 risk can be offset.

  • Nelson MR, et al. The APOE-R136S mutation protects against APOE4-driven Tau pathology, neurodegeneration and neuroinflammation. Nature Neuroscience 2023. Read

    The Christchurch change blocks APOE4-driven tau damage in mice, supporting APOE-directed therapies.

Targeting APOE

  • Huynh TV, et al. Age-dependent effects of apoE reduction using antisense oligonucleotides in a model of β-amyloidosis. Neuron 2017. Read

    Lowering APOE before plaques form strongly reduces amyloid pathology in mice; later lowering changes plaque size and toxicity.

  • Liao F, et al. Targeting of nonlipidated, aggregated apoE with antibodies inhibits amyloid accumulation. Journal of Clinical Investigation 2018. Read

    The HAE-4 antibody, which binds aggregated apoE, lowers plaque burden in APOE4 mice.

Reviews

  • Belloy ME, Napolioni V, Greicius MD. A quarter century of APOE and Alzheimer’s disease: progress to date and the path forward. Neuron 2019. Read

    Broad review of APOE genetics and biology.

  • Yamazaki Y, Zhao N, Caulfield TR, Liu CC, Bu G. Apolipoprotein E and Alzheimer disease: pathobiology and targeting strategies. Nature Reviews Neurology 2019. Read

    Mechanisms and therapeutic approaches.

  • Hampel H, Elhage A, Cho M, Apostolova LG, Nicoll JAR, Atri A. Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities (ARIA): radiological, biological and clinical characteristics. Brain 2023. Read

    What ARIA is, how it is detected, and why ε4/ε4 raises risk.

Community and policy

  • UsAgainstAlzheimer’s. FDA Patient Listening Session: Alzheimer’s disease and APOE ε4 homozygotes. Summary, July 2025. Read

    APOE ε4 homozygotes describe their needs and the lack of treatment options to FDA staff.

Trial results by genotype

  • van Dyck CH, Swanson CJ, Aisen P, et al. Lecanemab in early Alzheimer’s disease. New England Journal of Medicine 2023. Read

    Clarity AD; ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

  • Sims JR, Zimmer JA, Evans CD, et al. Donanemab in early symptomatic Alzheimer disease: the TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 randomized clinical trial. JAMA 2023. Read

    ARIA rates reported by APOE genotype.

  • Efficacy and safety of oral valiltramiprosate in APOE4/4 homozygotes with early AD: topline results from the APOLLOE4 Phase 3 trial. 2025. Read

    The first completed Phase 3 trial run only in ε4/ε4.
