Glossary

Terms used on this site

Short definitions of the genetics, biomarker and trial terms that come up in APOE4 research.

Allele
One version of a gene. APOE has three common alleles: ε2, ε3 and ε4.
Homozygote / heterozygote
A homozygote carries two copies of the same allele (ε4/ε4). A heterozygote carries two different alleles (for example ε3/ε4).
Penetrance
The share of people with a genotype who develop the disease. ε4/ε4 shows high but incomplete penetrance.
Amyloid-β (Aβ)
A protein fragment that clumps into plaques between brain cells; the earliest known change in Alzheimer’s.
Tau
A protein that forms tangles inside neurons. Tau spread tracks closely with symptoms.
Cerebral amyloid angiopathy (CAA)
Amyloid deposited in the walls of brain blood vessels, which weakens them and raises the risk of bleeds. More common with ε4.
Microbleed
A tiny, usually silent bleed in the brain, seen on certain MRI scans.
ARIA
Amyloid-related imaging abnormalities: brain swelling (ARIA-E) or small bleeds and iron deposits (ARIA-H) seen on MRI, mainly with anti-amyloid antibodies. Risk is highest in ε4/ε4.
Amyloid PET / tau PET
Brain scans that measure amyloid plaques or tau tangles in living people.
Plasma biomarkers
Blood tests for Alzheimer’s-related proteins, such as p-tau217, Aβ42/40, NfL and GFAP.
MCI
Mild cognitive impairment: measurable decline in memory or thinking that does not yet disrupt daily life.
CDR-SB, ADAS-Cog, iADRS
Rating scales used as outcomes in Alzheimer’s trials to measure cognition and daily function.
Preclinical Alzheimer’s
Alzheimer’s brain changes, such as amyloid, without symptoms. Prevention trials enroll at this stage.
Natural history study
An observational study that follows people over time without an experimental treatment, to learn how a disease unfolds.
Gene therapy
Delivering genetic material, often with a harmless virus (AAV), to change what cells produce, for example adding APOE2.
APOE3 Christchurch
A rare APOE variant that appeared to protect a carrier of an early-onset Alzheimer’s mutation, pointing to APOE as a drug target.
BACE inhibitor
A drug that blocks the enzyme BACE1, which cuts the amyloid precursor protein to make amyloid-β.
Antisense oligonucleotide (ASO)
A short strand of synthetic genetic material that lowers production of a specific protein, such as APOE.
LRRK2
A kinase enzyme involved in inflammation and cell trafficking; the target of the investigational drug HT-4253.
Odds ratio
How much more likely an outcome is in one group than another; 1 means no difference.
FDA Fast Track
A US designation that speeds review of drugs for serious conditions with unmet need. It is not an approval.
