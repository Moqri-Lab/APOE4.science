About this site and its sources

What APOE4.science is, who maintains it, and how information is chosen and checked.

Who maintains it

APOE4.science is maintained by Mahdi Moqri, PhD, in a personal capacity. It is not an official resource of any employer or institution, and it is not medical advice.

How sources are chosen

  • Every figure links to its source, preferring peer-reviewed papers, trial registries and official drug labels.
  • Trial results are shown as reported; company announcements and subgroup findings are labeled as such.
  • Trial statuses are checked against ClinicalTrials.gov. The “As of” date shows when each result or status applies.
  • Where studies disagree, both are listed (for example, on blood biomarkers in Papers).

Corrections and updates

Found an error or a missing study? Suggest a correction on GitHub, where the full source and change history are public. For research updates, subscribe to the newsletter.
