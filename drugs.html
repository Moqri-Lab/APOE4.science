Drugs

Treatments and what they mean for ε4/ε4

APOE genotype matters most for the safety of anti-amyloid antibodies. Several newer approaches target APOE4 biology directly. Within each group, the most advanced or most recent come first.

Approved for early Alzheimer’s

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Lecanemab (Leqembi)Anti-amyloid antibody; IV, with a weekly under-the-skin autoinjector (Leqembi Iqlik) approved in the US in 2025 for maintenance after 18 monthsHigher risk of ARIA, including symptomatic and serious ARIA, in ε4/ε4. In the US, a boxed warning applies and APOE testing is advised before treatment; patients who decline testing can still be treated. In the EU (April 2025), approved only for people who are not ε4 homozygotes. ↗ ↗
Donanemab (Kisunla)Anti-amyloid antibody, IVHigher risk of ARIA in ε4/ε4. In the US, a boxed warning applies, APOE testing is advised before treatment, and extra vigilance for ARIA is advised in the first 24 weeks. The FDA approved a slower dose titration that lowered ARIA-E, most in ε4/ε4 (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 6). In the EU (September 2025), approved only for people who are not ε4 homozygotes. ↗ ↗ ↗
Donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine, memantineSymptomatic treatmentsTreat symptoms; do not slow the disease. Used regardless of APOE genotype. ↗

Investigational, aimed at ε4/ε4

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Valiltramiprosate (ALZ-801)Oral; blocks formation of toxic amyloid oligomersPhase 3 APOLLOE4 in ε4/ε4 missed its primary endpoint; a prespecified MCI subgroup favored drug. Low ARIA. ↗
HT-4253Oral LRRK2 inhibitor acting on the RAB10 pathwayPhase 2a prevention trial in biomarker-positive ε4 carriers, now recruiting in Abu Dhabi, UAE.
LX1001Gene therapy that adds the protective APOE2 formPhase 1/2 in ε4/ε4; early biomarker signals, no ARIA reported. FDA Fast Track. ↗

Earlier-stage approaches

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Anti-APOE antibodiesAntibodies against aggregated apoE, such as HAE-4Lower amyloid plaques in APOE4 mice; not yet in clinical trials as far as published reports show. ↗
APOE4 loweringAntisense oligonucleotides and gene silencingAim to reduce APOE production; lowering APOE before plaques form reduced amyloid in mice. Preclinical or early development. ↗
APOE2 plus APOE4 silencingGene therapy (for example Lexeo’s LX1020)Adds protective APOE2 while suppressing APOE4; preclinical. ↗
Gene editingCRISPR-based conversion of APOE4Proof-of-concept studies only, such as prime editing of APOE4 in mice with brain-directed AAV; efficiency must improve before clinical use. ↗
