Drugs

Treatments and what they mean for ε4/ε4

APOE genotype matters most for the safety of anti-amyloid antibodies. Several newer approaches target APOE4 biology directly.

Approved for early Alzheimer’s

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Lecanemab (Leqembi)Anti-amyloid antibody, IV or subcutaneousHigher risk of ARIA in ε4/ε4. In the US, a boxed warning applies and APOE testing is advised before treatment. In the EU (April 2025), approved only for people who are not ε4 homozygotes.
Donanemab (Kisunla)Anti-amyloid antibody, IVHigher risk of ARIA in ε4/ε4. The FDA approved a slower dose titration that lowered ARIA-E, most in ε4/ε4 (TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 6). In the EU (September 2025), approved only for people who are not ε4 homozygotes.
Donepezil, rivastigmine, galantamine, memantineSymptomatic treatmentsTreat symptoms; do not slow the disease. Used regardless of APOE genotype.

Investigational, aimed at ε4/ε4

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Valiltramiprosate (ALZ-801)Oral; blocks formation of toxic amyloid oligomersPhase 3 APOLLOE4 in ε4/ε4 missed its primary endpoint; a prespecified MCI subgroup favored drug. Low ARIA.
LX1001Gene therapy that adds the protective APOE2 formPhase 1/2 in ε4/ε4; early biomarker signals, no ARIA in interim data. FDA Fast Track.
HT-4253Oral LRRK2 inhibitor acting on the RAB10 pathwayPhase 2a prevention trial in biomarker-positive ε4 carriers planned; not yet recruiting.

Stopped or withdrawn

DrugTypeFor ε4/ε4
Aducanumab (Aduhelm)Anti-amyloid antibodyDiscontinued by the manufacturer in 2024.
Umibecestat (CNP520)BACE inhibitorStopped in 2019 in Generation Studies after mild cognitive worsening.
CAD106Active amyloid vaccineGeneration Study 1 arm halted in 2019 with the rest of the program.
