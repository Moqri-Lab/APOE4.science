For researchers

Where to find ε4/ε4 data

Cohorts and repositories with APOE genotype linked to clinical, biomarker, omic or autopsy data.

ResourceWhat it offers for APOE4 researchAccess
NACCClinical follow-up and neuropathology from US Alzheimer’s Disease Research Centers, with APOE genotype for most participants. Basis of the Fortea 2024 analysis.Online request form
NIAGADS / ADSPGenome-wide and whole-genome sequencing data, including more diverse ancestries. Can be linked to NACC records.Formal data access request
NCRADStored DNA, plasma, serum and spinal fluid from research-center participants with known outcomes.Biosample request
ADNIRepeated amyloid and tau PET, MRI, fluid biomarkers and cognition, with APOE genotype.Data use agreement
A4 / LEARNCognitively unimpaired older adults with amyloid PET, repeated MRI including microbleeds, and APOE genotype.Data use agreement
AD Knowledge PortalBrain multi-omics by genotype (ROSMAP, Mount Sinai, Mayo) and APOE4 knock-in mouse models (MODEL-AD).Synapse account and data use certificate
UK BiobankAbout half a million participants with genotypes, plasma proteomics and long-term health records, including a large ε4/ε4 group.Approved project application

Each resource sets its own terms. NIH rules on controlled-access genomic data also limit the use of public generative AI tools with individual-level data.
